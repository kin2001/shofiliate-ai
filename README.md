# Shofiliate AI

**Smarter Campaigns. Powered by AI.**

## Overview

Shofiliate AI is a planned AI-powered affiliate marketing platform that helps affiliates analyze products, create campaign content, review and approve campaigns, publish to Facebook, and track campaign performance. It aims to simplify the workflow from product discovery to campaign insights while keeping users in control of what gets published.

## The Problem

Affiliate marketers often move between separate tools to research products, write promotional content, publish posts, and review results. This manual process takes time and makes it harder to connect campaign decisions with performance.

Shofiliate AI aims to bring these steps into one guided workflow, using AI to help users prepare campaigns while keeping review and publishing decisions in their hands.

## Who It Is For

- Affiliate marketers who want help turning product information into campaign drafts.
- Content creators who promote products through Facebook.
- Small marketing teams that need a shared review and approval process.

## Planned Features

- **AI product analysis:** Analyze products to identify selling points and campaign opportunities.
- **AI campaign generation:** Generate campaign ideas and promotional content.
- **Campaign review and approval:** Review and approve campaigns before publishing.
- **Facebook publishing:** Publish approved campaign content to Facebook.
- **Click tracking:** Track clicks on affiliate links.
- **Performance analytics:** Review campaign results and engagement insights.

## Planned Campaign Workflow

1. **Add a product:** Provide product information and an affiliate link.
2. **Analyze the product:** Use AI to suggest selling points, audience ideas, and promotional angles based on the supplied information.
3. **Generate a campaign:** Create draft captions and calls to action for review.
4. **Review and approve:** Check product claims, edit the content, and approve the final campaign.
5. **Publish to Facebook:** Publish approved content through an authorized Facebook connection, subject to the permissions required by Meta.
6. **Measure results:** Track affiliate-link clicks and review available campaign metrics to inform the next campaign.

## Planned AI Approach

Meta AI models are intended to support product analysis and campaign content generation. The specific models and integration approach will be selected during hackathon development.

AI-generated content will be treated as a draft. Users will be responsible for checking accuracy, affiliate disclosures, and suitability before approval and publishing. Click tracking is intended to measure link activity; it does not by itself verify purchases or affiliate earnings.

## Development Priorities

- Keep API keys and service credentials on the server and out of the public repository.
- Limit access to campaign data and connected accounts to authorized users.
- Make campaign status clear so users can distinguish drafts, approved content, and published posts.
- Show available performance data without presenting estimates as confirmed sales or earnings.
